Scatter plot of imp_r to imp_i coverage ratio against corpus size for twenty corpora. The nineteen fragmented book corpora fall along a declining trend from about 0.99 at 944 rows to 0.72 at 129,000 rows. French Proust sits above the trend at 1.09. WikiText-103, prepared as real sentences, sits far above at 1.44 despite being the largest corpus at 866,000 rows.
